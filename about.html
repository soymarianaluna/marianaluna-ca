About me

Hi! I’m Maru, a brand strategist and communications leader based in Calgary, Alberta.

For 13 years I have helped organizations across Latin America and North America find clarity, build identity, and communicate with intention. Startups, multinationals, nonprofits, venture firms, agricultural giants and biotech companies: 70+ brands, 12 rebranding processes, and marketing budgets from $5K to $750K USD.

My work moves from strategy to execution. From brand architecture to PR and digital marketing, I have done all of it, though brand identity, storytelling and copywriting are where I do my best work.

Born and raised in Argentina, I became a digital nomad in 2019 and have lived in four countries across the Americas since. An early adopter of AI and new tech, I treat communication as a social science: complex, inexact, exciting.

Today I focus on mission driven organizations. The ones building something that outlasts a campaign, a quarter or a trend, where communication is not a function but part of the mission itself.

Forbes Argentina contributor. Bilingual English and Spanish. My motto: start with a question.

Mindset and approach

  • Communication is a social science: complex, inexact, exciting.
