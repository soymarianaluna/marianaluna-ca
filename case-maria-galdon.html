Family businesses tend to hit the same wall as they grow: the brand was built for one business, and success turned them into several.

Visit the site

Context

María knew this. She did not say it that way. What she said was that opportunities kept slipping past because everything sat under a single name, covering activities that complemented each other without being the same thing.

There was a second point, and it mattered more. Demand already exceeded her capacity, and she knew the rebrand had to say so. We were opening a boutique, not a mall.

What I did

I brought the client in myself and partnered with Funka Brands to deliver the project. Then we did the part we like best: pure strategy. Listening closely, asking sharply, interpreting carefully, and making the whole thing add up.

I led the team, a project manager, a creative director, a graphic designer and a web developer, and stayed the single point of contact throughout. María is based in Calgary as well, so much of this happened in the same room.

I designed and ran every strategy workshop, interview and benchmark, and defined the brand's voice and key messages.

Results

In numbers

85%

average project ticket increase in the 18 months following launch

In meaning

Top of mind for the region's most demanding projects.

Exactly the tier the repositioning was built to reach.

In trust

They came back for the parent brand.

Planta Landscape, the family business this brand separated from, hired us to rebrand it too. María and I are still working together in 2026.
